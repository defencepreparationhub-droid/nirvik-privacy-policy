Account and Data Deletion

NIRVIK Defence Preparation provides users with an option to delete their account through the App's Profile section. This page explains how to request account and associated data deletion.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the NIRVIK Defence Preparation app.
  2. Log in using your registered mobile number and password.
  3. Open the Profile section.
  4. Select the Delete Account option.
  5. Follow the instructions shown in the App to complete the request.
Important: Account deletion may permanently remove your account and associated information. Review any confirmation message shown in the App before completing the deletion.

Data That May Be Deleted

When an account deletion is completed, account-related information under our control may include:

  • User account and profile information
  • Registered mobile number and authentication-related account data
  • Practice activity and examination results associated with the account
  • Premium access information associated with the account, where applicable
  • Support information associated with the account, where applicable

The specific information deleted depends on the data associated with the account and the App's actual deletion process.

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained for a limited period when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or other legitimate obligations.

Any retained information should be kept only for as long as necessary for the applicable purpose and handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

Deletion Processing

The App may require confirmation before completing an account deletion to help prevent unauthorized deletion. Follow the confirmation steps shown in the App.

If your account cannot be accessed or the Delete Account option does not work, contact the developer using the contact information provided on the App's Google Play Store listing.
