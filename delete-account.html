Account Deletion Request

NIRVIK Defence Preparation respects your privacy and provides users with a way to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Open the NIRVIK Defence Preparation app.
  2. Log in using your registered mobile number and password.
  3. Go to the app's Help & Support section.
  4. Submit a request stating that you want your account and associated data deleted.
  5. Include your registered mobile number so that we can identify the account.
Important: Please make sure that the mobile number you provide matches the mobile number registered with your NIRVIK Defence Preparation account.

Data That Will Be Deleted

When an account deletion request is processed, the following user account-related data will be deleted where applicable:

  • User account information
  • Registered mobile number and authentication-related account data
  • User profile information
  • Practice and examination results associated with the account
  • Premium access information associated with the account
  • Support information associated with the account, where applicable

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained for a limited period when necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or other legitimate legal obligations.

Such information will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the applicable purpose and will be deleted when it is no longer required.

Deletion Processing

Account deletion requests are reviewed before processing to help prevent unauthorized deletion of an account. Once verified, the applicable account and associated data will be deleted from our active systems.

Need Help?

If you cannot access your account, you may submit an account deletion request through the available support method provided in the NIRVIK Defence Preparation app or its Google Play Store listing.
