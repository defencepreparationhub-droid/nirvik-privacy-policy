Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 12, 2026

NIRVIK Defence Preparation ("we", "our", or "us") operates the NIRVIK Defence Preparation mobile application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected or processed, how it is used, how it is protected, and how users can request deletion of their information.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect or process the following information:

  • Mobile number used to register or log in to the App, and user ID or authentication-related information.
  • Practice answers, examination results, scores, and time used for educational and examination features.
  • Premium access status, activation details, and expiration information, where applicable.
  • Support requests and information provided when users contact us.
  • Device type, operating system, app version, and technical diagnostic information, where processed.
  • Advertising identifiers and related device information, where applicable to advertising services.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create, authenticate, and manage user accounts.
  • Provide educational content, practice questions, and examinations.
  • Save and display examination results and progress.
  • Manage premium access and expiration information, where applicable.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve App performance.
  • Help detect unauthorized access, misuse, and technical problems.
  • Display and measure advertisements, where applicable.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Firebase Services

The App uses Google Firebase services to support authentication, cloud data storage, and backend functionality.

Depending on the features used, information processed through Firebase may include account identifiers, authentication-related information, examination data, premium access information, and support-related information.

Firebase may process or store information to provide these services on behalf of the App. Such processing is subject to the applicable Firebase service terms and privacy documentation.

4. Advertising

The App may display advertisements using Google AdMob when advertising features are enabled.

Google AdMob may process advertising identifiers, device-related information, and other information necessary to provide, measure, and improve advertising services. Advertising practices may depend on the services enabled and the applicable settings.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, visit Google's Privacy Policy .

5. Data Storage and Security

Some information generated through the App may be processed or stored using Google Firebase cloud services.

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Appropriate access restrictions and security settings should be maintained for the services used by the App.

Access to personal information should be limited to purposes necessary to operate, maintain, and support the App.

No method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate the App, including Google Firebase and, where advertising is enabled, Google AdMob.

We may disclose information when required by applicable law or valid legal process, or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of users or the App.

7. Children's Privacy

NIRVIK Defence Preparation is an educational and examination preparation application intended for general users.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact the developer using the contact details provided on the App's Google Play Store listing.

8. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services, including Google Firebase and Google AdMob. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies, terms, and settings.

We encourage users to review the relevant third-party privacy information to understand how those services handle information.

9. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App's services, maintain account and examination records, administer premium access, respond to support requests, maintain security, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer needed, appropriate steps should be taken to delete it or otherwise handle it in accordance with applicable requirements. Some information may remain in backups or records for a limited period where necessary.

10. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their account and associated personal information by following the instructions on our Account Deletion page or by contacting the developer through the contact details provided on the Google Play Store listing.

We will review and process deletion requests for information under our control, subject to applicable legal obligations and legitimate security requirements.

Information retained by third-party service providers may also be subject to their applicable retention and deletion procedures.

11. Your Choices and Privacy Requests

Users may contact the developer through the Google Play Store listing to ask questions about personal information, request account or data deletion, or raise a privacy concern.

Depending on the features available in the App and applicable law, users may also have choices regarding account information, advertising settings, and the use of App features.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its services, or applicable requirements change. When changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of NIRVIK Defence Preparation, please contact the developer using the contact information provided on the application's Google Play Store listing.
